12.3 About this wiki

This wiki is a synthesis and does not present original findings. Where it extends beyond the source papers — worked examples, comparison tables, checklists, reviewer scripts — that material is shown in a Wiki Tip box. Corrections are welcome; see Contribute.

The three source papers are linked by DOI above, in The source papers. The SORTEE guidelines paper is open access under CC BY 4.0.
