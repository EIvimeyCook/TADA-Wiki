11 References

Sources cited in this wiki, together with the key works the three source papers draw on. Bibliographic details follow the reference lists of those papers; where a source gives an abbreviated form, that form is reproduced here rather than completed by guesswork.

DOIs are given for the three source papers and wherever the source reference lists supply one. Many entries below still lack a DOI that does exist; adding them is a standing task, and contributions are welcome.

11.3 About this wiki

This wiki is a synthesis and does not present original findings. Where it extends beyond the source papers — worked examples, comparison tables, checklists, reviewer scripts — that material is labelled wiki guidance. Corrections are welcome; see Contribute.

The three source PDFs are in papers/, with their terms of use. The SORTEE guidelines paper is open access under CC BY 4.0.
